# Principles — content-factory (deltas)

Follows the **skandar base**: `[[vlad-vault/principles|vlad-vault/principles]]` (object rules)
+ `[[vlad-vault/vault-template|vlad-vault/vault-template]]` (structure & entry points). Only deltas
here.

## Deltas
- **Separate venture, not maksi-studio.** Carries the `maksi-` name as a brand branch but
  is shared (Skandar + Adil); maksi-studio automation stays Skandar's undiluted. Never
  fold this venture's clients/IP into maksi-studio or vice-versa.
- **No deal terms in files.** Equity, splits, who-gets-what = Skandar's human matter, kept
  out of tracked files (pointers only). Same as the Aigul doc discipline.
- **No personal data.** Adil's / clients' private contacts, numbers — public source only,
  pointer not value.
- **Language.** English for infra/canon (this file, HOME, status, structure); Russian for
  verbatim audience-facing copy (offers, scripts, pricing lines). EN-infra / RU-content
  axis.
- **Adil is origination, never the delivery critical path.** Architecture rule, not a
  preference: nothing shippable may depend on his follow-through.
- **Освоение через чужое сначала, свои форматы — потом.** Новый видео-паттерн берём
  разбором чужого референса (`../../intake/ref/`, teardown по осям станка) и **пытаемся
  воспроизвести** нашим стеком, чтобы освоить. Свои форматы придумываем на уже освоенной
  базе, не с нуля. Правило метода (Скандар 2026-07-06), не разовость.
