# Positioning — content-factory

Concept-level. The offer, the pricing ladder, and why it has a moat. Copy is non-final.

## The wedge
Not "another SMM agency". The offer is **"content from the studio of the guy who
personally pulls 300–560K views"** (Adil, @eliminate_kz, ~72K followers, reels regularly
40–130K and spiking 290–560K). His own numbers ARE the portfolio. No competitor in
Astana can show that the face/founder personally pulls those figures.

## The hidden engine (the real moat)
Each client gets a small **per-client knowledge base** (brand, tone, products, dos/don'ts)
so the AI produces *grounded* content, not generic slop. This is the agentic / LLM-wiki
substance Skandar already owns, working invisibly under a mass-market service the buyer
never sees as "AI infrastructure". Margin comes the same way infocygane get it: near-zero
marginal cost of content (AI) + distribution that already exists (Adil).

## The pricing ladder (concept) — the factory is a downsell of Adil's face
- **Tier 1 — Adil personally:** ~1M ₸/mo. His time, premium, scarce, low volume.
- **Tier 2 — "моя маркетинговая компания":** ~200K ₸/mo — 1 personal Adil reel/mo (the
  credibility anchor) + the factory (templated-Adil + AI) for the rest.
- **Tier 3 (optional) — pure factory, no Adil:** cheapest, for who can't afford Tier 2.

The mechanic: every "дорого" on the 1M offer does not walk away — it converts down to the
200K factory. **Adil's face is the top-of-funnel ad for the factory.** Bundling his
persona with the factory is the moat; a competitor can sell AI content but cannot sell
*his* proof.

## Two ICPs note
This venture is the **mass-market / hype-OK** side of the house. The serious anti-hype
B2B automation offer stays separate under Skandar / maksi
(`~/skandar/skandar-vault/ideas/2026-06-08-skandar-level-services.md` (машина Скандара)). Do not let the
factory's tone contaminate the B2B positioning.
