# Market overview — content-factory

Concept-level. Demand source and competitive frame.

## Demand source (the asset)
Adil (@eliminate_kz) is flooded with inbound: restaurants, brands, local businesses
wanting SMM / reels / promotion. Today this demand is **wasted** — he takes only what
interests him and abandons the rest. The venture's primary input is this **existing,
already-warm overflow** routed into a system instead of the floor.

Adil's reach as the demand magnet (observed 2026-06-26):
- ~72.1K followers; reels regularly 40–130K views, spiking 290–560K.
- Commercial standing: ambassador (@piro.rest, @miss_student_astana), production
  (@bpc.qz, @kumystan_show), event host (ShowMan), national-brand contract ("Лицо ЕНПФ").

## Competitive frame
- Generic SMM agencies / freelancers: sell content, but cannot show a face that
  personally pulls 300–560K views. The factory's proof object is Adil himself.
- The AI-content angle alone is commoditising fast; the durable moat is **Adil's proof +
  the per-client knowledge base** (grounded output, not generic), not "we use AI".
